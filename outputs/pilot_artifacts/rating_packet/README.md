# Blind image ratings

Each C-number JPG shows the source, the instruction, and one edited candidate. Rate each candidate independently. Fill requested_change, preservation, and usability with 1 (yes) or 0 (no). Do not look at rating_key.csv until both forms are complete. Rater 1 and rater 2 should be different people; at least one must not be the output runner. Send the completed CSVs back for consolidation. If any component disagrees, a third person rates that candidate without seeing prior votes.
